# 🪂 The Alchemy Airdrop

## The Fourth Game

The **OG Airdrop** is the highly anticipated fourth game of the Realm — the engine of distribution, seeded and waiting. It has a name and a date:

> **The Alchemy Airdrop — UEC Epoch 904 — December 25, 2026.**

On that day, in a single act, the OG Airdrop will distribute a substantial portion of the [$OGA](../physics/tokens/oga.md) supply — the dark science itself — to the citizens of the Realm.

## Who Qualifies

**Citizens.** The floor is the [OG gate](../society/citizenship.md) — 100M $OGC liquid, or any higher Class. No registration exists; the chain already knows who you are.

## How the Realm Judges

The Realm will judge **its own gameboard and history** — not a snapshot, not a screenshot, not a form. The record that decides is the one the chain has kept all along: who arrived (your [Birth Epoch](../realm/how-to-enter.md)), who stayed, who played, and how deeply. The Realm rewards those it has known best and longest.

{% hint style="warning" %}
The allocation's exact formula is sealed, and will be revealed in the Mad OG's time. What is already certain: **history cannot be purchased retroactively.** Every Epoch between now and UEC 904 is either on your record or not.
{% endhint %}

## What the Alchemy Moves

The event reshapes the Realm's society in a single day:

* **500,000 $OGA** stands ready in the Airdrop's keeping — the whole of it supplied from the Crown's own [Lab](../power/og-lab.md), which was endowed 80% of the supply, passed this 50% to the Airdrop, and retains 30% (see [The Crown's Weight](../society/classes.md))
* The [Noble gate](../society/classes.md) is 10,000 $OGA — the Alchemy can mint up to **50 new Noble seats** into a class that, by the scarcity of its sworn token, has barely been populated
* [Knights](../society/knighthood.md) carry $OGA allocations among their standing perks
