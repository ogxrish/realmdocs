## What Citizens Are

* **Counted.** Citizens stand in the Realm's census — ranked, classed, and remembered by [Birth Epoch](../realm/how-to-enter.md).
* **Standing.** The 💎 OG is the common man of the Realm — and although there is a place for every OG, there are no equals. The ladder rises from here.
* **Eligible.** When the Realm rewards its own — as it will at the [Alchemy Airdrop](../era-2/alchemy-airdrop.md), UEC 904 — it is the citizens it judges, by their gameboard and their history.

## The Age of a Citizen

Your **Birth Epoch** — the UEC Epoch of your wallet's first qualifying touch of the Realm — is permanent. It cannot be purchased, reset, or transferred. Two citizens may hold identical bags; the chain will still know which of them was there first.

{% hint style="info" %}
The Realm judges by what it has recorded — not by what is claimed. Hold the floor, play the games, and the record builds itself.
{% endhint %}
