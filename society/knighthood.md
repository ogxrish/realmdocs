# 🛡️ Knighthood

## The Court of the Realm

The Knights are the court of the Realm — its recognized security, its senior and proven players, the standing closest to the throne. Knighthood is to Knights as Citizenship is to Citizens: not a trophy, but a standing, held only as long as it is kept.

The Knight's rank was announced with the Classes, late in the Creation Era. The Knights' role — their presence, their privileges, their part in the games still to come — is expected to grow through Era II.

## The Liquid Bond

A Knight holds, **simultaneously and liquid**:

> **1,000,000 $OGG + 1,000,000,000 $OGC + 1,000,000,000,000 $OGF**

Liquid means liquid. Gold locked in the Reserve does not count. The bond must stand in the open, visible to the chain at all times — and that discipline is not a technicality. **It is the point.** The court keeps its wealth where the Realm can see it; the keeping is the oath.

Let a component lapse and the rank rolls away. Restore it, and the rank returns. The chain is the herald.

## The Arithmetic of the Court

* Each requirement is **0.1% of its token's supply** — at most **1,000 Knights can ever exist**
* Every Knight seat is **triple-taxed**: it consumes a Capital seat, a Builder seat, and a Degen seat at once
* **11 Knights stand seated today** — the live count holds on the [Onchain Status](../canon/onchain-status.md) page

## The Perks

What the bond buys, beyond the rank itself:

* **Standing in the court** — the Knights are the Realm's recognized security and its senior players
* **$OGA allocations** — the court is served first from the dark science; the Alchemy thread runs through Knighthood
* **A seat at the Roundtable** — the court convenes; what passes there stays behind its doors
* **Priority commissions** — when the Realm has work, the court hears of it first

## The Costs

Capital, held open. Three tokens, balanced at once. A rank that rolls the day discipline lapses. The court is expensive by construction — that is why it is the court.
