# ♾️ Eternal Pools

## Established by OG Bank

Created by [OG Bank](../../power/og-bank.md), Eternal Pools are official pools in which 3 Realm tokens are permanently bonded to outer-world assets.

These pairs establish a relative common-asset association for the token order within the Realm — and they are the Realm's standing gates to the value of the outside world.

## Alignment

In addition to providing comparative value against commonly known metrics of the world outside the Realm's walls, the Eternal Pools allow for outside connections, flows, and influence relating to what matters for the Realm's OGs:

* 10% [$OGM](../tokens/ogm.md) **is permanently bonded to** cbBTC — the Realm's link to Bitcoin
* 10% [$OGG](../tokens/ogg.md) **is permanently bonded to** SOL — the Realm's link to its native chain
* 10% [$OGC](../tokens/ogc.md) **is permanently bonded to** USDC — the Realm's link to the dollar

{% hint style="warning" %}
$OGM, $OGG, $OGC have a total of 30% of their supply permanently bonded across Realm and Eternal Pools.
{% endhint %}
