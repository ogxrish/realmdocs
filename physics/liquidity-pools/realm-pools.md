# 🏰 Realm Pools

## Established by OG Bank

Created by [OG Bank](../../power/og-bank.md), Realm Pools are 5 official pools in which the 6 Realm [tokens](../tokens.md) are permanently bonded to each other.

These pairs define the structure of the token order within the Realm — the ladder made physical.

## Alignment

10% of each token supply was added to a Liquidity Pool with its 2 Realm pairs (with the exception of $OGR and $OGF, which only have 1 Realm pair) — **and that liquidity has been burned forever**:

* 10% [$OGR](../tokens/ogr.md) **is permanently bonded to** 10% [$OGM](../tokens/ogm.md)
* 10% [$OGM](../tokens/ogm.md) **is permanently bonded to** 10% [$OGA](../tokens/oga.md)
* 10% [$OGA](../tokens/oga.md) **is permanently bonded to** 10% [$OGG](../tokens/ogg.md)
* 10% [$OGG](../tokens/ogg.md) **is permanently bonded to** 10% [$OGC](../tokens/ogc.md)
* 10% [$OGC](../tokens/ogc.md) **is permanently bonded to** 10% [$OGF](../tokens/ogf.md)

{% hint style="warning" %}
$OGM, $OGA, $OGG, $OGC have a total of 20% of their supply permanently locked together in Realm Pools, while $OGR and $OGF have a total of 10% of their supply permanently locked in Realm Pools.
{% endhint %}

The creation and the burning of every bond is on the chain — see the [Timeline](../../realm/timeline.md) for every transaction.
