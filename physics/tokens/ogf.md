## Purpose

$OGF is the primal degeneracy of the Realm. One quadrillion units of luck, fate, and foolish gambles — the token of the [OG Lottery](../../games/og-lottery.md), where bids accumulate into pools and the Final Bid takes the lion's share.

The Fool is the only Realm token that **burns**. Unclaimed winnings burn. Repurchased supply burns. The Fool's destiny is to grow scarcer through its own recklessness — and every burn permanently destroys future [Degen seats](../../society/classes.md).
