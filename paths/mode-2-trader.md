# 📊 Mode 2 · The Trader

Six tokens, one geometric ladder, eighteen regulated pools, three outer-world anchors. The Realm is a small economy with visible plumbing — and for a trader, visible plumbing is opportunity.

## The Instruments

* **The rungs** — each [Realm Pool](../physics/liquidity-pools/realm-pools.md) bonds two adjacent tokens (OGR–OGM–OGA–OGG–OGC–OGF). The ladder's relative prices breathe as the games emit and players climb.
* **The anchors** — the [Eternal Pools](../physics/liquidity-pools/eternal-pools.md) bond the Realm to cbBTC, SOL, and USDC. When the outer world moves, the anchors transmit it inward — unevenly, and lag is a trade.
* **The SOL gates** — every token's [Sanctioned Pools](../physics/liquidity-pools/sanctioned-pools.md) exist in V1 (0.25%) and V2 (1%) — two venues, one pair, with flow that splits between them.

## What a Trader Reads

* **Emission pressure** — the Mine pays miners daily; the Reserve pays 274M $OGC daily; the Lottery floods $OGF in growing waves. Sellers of emissions are structural; their cadence is public.
* **Repurchase bids** — Game fees buy $OGG, $OGC, $OGF back from the market on visible wallets ([watch them](../games/repurchase-programs.md)).
* **Threshold demand** — every [Class quota](../society/classes.md) is a standing buy-wall in waiting: a wallet approaching a gate must finish the climb.
* **Event horizons** — UEC 904 approaches, and the [$OGA float](../physics/tokens/oga.md) is small and counted. Era II has more such dates ahead.

## The Trader's Edge in the Realm

Arbitrage between rungs, between V1/V2 venues, between anchor lag and ladder truth — all of it is legal: *there are laws, and portals, and loopholes in the Realm, and everything is fair game for OGs.* The Realm prices itself; the trader argues with the price.
