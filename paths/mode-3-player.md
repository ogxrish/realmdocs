# Mode 3 · The Player

The games are where the Realm's value enters circulation — and where daily discipline compounds into fortune. This is the mode of the practitioner.

## The Daily Loop

1. **Mine early.** The [Mine's](../games/og-mine.md) fee follows the [one difficulty law](../physics/difficulty.md): gentle to the early, steep for the crowd. In an uncrowded Epoch every miner pays pennies; the earliest pay least. Then claim within 10 Epochs, always.
2. **Allocate deliberately.** The [Reserve](../games/og-reserve.md) is a fresh four-way standoff every Epoch: lock $OGG once, then allocate to Alpha, Beta, Gamma, or Delta — chasing the slot that finishes **second**.
3. **Bid with intent.** The [Lottery](../games/og-lottery.md) rewards three temperaments — early (cheap claims on half the pool), late (the Final Bid's 50%), and vigilant (bid **count** sets your share).

## The Player's Laws

* **Streaks compound.** Mine emissions decay 1% per Epoch — Gold not mined today is mined cheaper by no one, ever. The chain remembers unbroken runs.
* **Claims expire.** All three games return or burn unclaimed rewards after 10 Epochs. Discipline is half the game.
* **Fees are information.** A high fee means a crowded period — which is itself a signal about where the herd stands.
* **Locked is not classed.** Gold in the Reserve earns $OGC but sheds [Class weight](../society/classes.md). Decide what each million of $OGG is *for*.

## Where It Leads

The player's bag diversifies by itself: Gold from the Mine, Coin from the Reserve, Fool from the pools. Classes follow ([Capital](../society/classes.md) 💰, Builder 🧱, Degen 🎲) — and the threefold bag is the raw material of the [Knight's bond](knights-path.md).
