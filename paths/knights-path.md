# 🛡️ The Knight's Path

Most OGs climb. Some arrive. The Knight's Path is the Realm's door for those who come with capital and intent — and choose to enter at the level of the court.

## The Bond, Assembled

[Knighthood](../society/knighthood.md) asks one thing, held liquid and simultaneous:

> **1,000,000 $OGG · 1,000,000,000 $OGC · 1,000,000,000,000 $OGF**

All three legs trade on the open market through the [Regulated Pools](../physics/liquidity-pools.md); the bond's live cost moves with the ladder and stands quoted on the [Onchain Status](../canon/onchain-status.md) page. Assembling it is itself a strategy:

* **Buy the legs** — fastest; the pools price your urgency
* **Earn the legs** — mine the Gold, farm the Coin, campaign the [Lottery](../games/og-lottery.md) for the Fool; slower, cheaper, and every step is recorded history
* **Blend** — most of the court did both

## What It Buys

Standing in the Mad OG's court. **$OGA allocations** — the Alchemy thread, served first to Knights as the [UEC 904 event](../era-2/alchemy-airdrop.md) approaches. A seat at the **Roundtable**. **Priority commissions** when the Realm has work. And the rank's arithmetic itself: one of at most **1,000 seats that can ever exist** — *11 are taken.*

## What It Demands

The bond stays liquid, visible, and whole — stake the Gold or spend a leg below its line, and the rank rolls away until restored. Each Knight seat also consumes a Capital, Builder, and Degen seat at once: **the court is triple-taxed by construction.** The discipline is the oath; the visibility is the point.

## The Loop

Bond → court → Alchemy → deeper stake. The Knight enters holding three tokens, is served $OGA among the first, and stands closest to whatever Era II reveals above the Noble's gate. For those reading the [ladder's silence](../society/classes.md) carefully: proximity to the court has never been only about the perks already named.

{% hint style="warning" %}
Some Knights will become drunks, and some Knights will become Lords. The bond buys the seat — what is done from it is the actual game.
{% endhint %}
