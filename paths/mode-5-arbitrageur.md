# Mode 5 · The Arbitrageur

Hard mode. Everything below is legal, onchain, and already being done by someone quieter than you. The Realm teaches the mechanics of success transparently — mastery is in the execution.

## The Difficulty Window

The [one difficulty law](../physics/difficulty.md) makes **position within the period** a tradeable edge: the first hundred actors of any Epoch pay pennies; crowds pay dearly (the fee grows with the square of the count). The arbitrage: act where the count is low. Empty Epochs are discounts; crowded ones are someone else's bill.

## The Second-Slot Game

The [Reserve](../games/og-reserve.md) pays the **second-largest** slot — a standoff with no stable script. The known plays:

* **The late read** — allocations land all Epoch; those who commit last see the board best. Difficulty prices that privilege: late means more counts ahead of you.
* **The weight play** — a large reserver doesn't predict the second slot; they **make** one. Land enough Gold on a trailing slot late, and second place is manufactured.
* **The spread** — 25/25/25/25 guarantees a share of second, forever mediocre. The defensive floor exists to be farmed by those who guess.
* **The herd fade** — when one slot becomes obvious, it finishes first and pays nothing. The prize lives one step from consensus.

## The Silence Game

The [Lottery](../games/og-lottery.md) ends on a soundless Epoch — so the Final Bid is a bet on *everyone else's absence*. The plays: bid-count compounding early (cheap claims on half the pool while the curve is low — it resets only on a win); pot-odds patience as the pool's accumulated $OGF grows against the climbing fee; and the closer's read — quiet hours, exhausted bidders, a fee already too steep for casual snipers. In the Creation Era, one player took 551 of 869 bids and opened 7 of 9 pools: vigilance, industrialized.

## Cross-Engine Loops

The engines feed each other, and a position can travel the whole circuit: mine Gold cheap in empty Epochs → lock it for $OGC yield (the Reserve's river) → fund Coin-denominated thresholds or Lottery campaigns with the yield → claims and prizes flow back through the [pools](../physics/liquidity-pools.md) you may also be lording. Each junction has a fee, a timing, and a class consequence — the loop is one machine to those who run it whole.

## Threshold & Horizon Plays

* **Quota races** — every [Class gate](../society/classes.md) is a finish line with countable runners; approaching wallets are forced buyers
* **The Noble float** — ~1% of $OGA crowns a Noble, the float is small and counted, and UEC 904 mints up to 50 new seats in a day. The race is won before the gun.
* **Seat deflation** — every $OGF burn retires Degen seats forever; scarcity has a schedule
* **Era horizons** — the [Alchemy Airdrop](../era-2/alchemy-airdrop.md) judges history; history is accumulated *before* events, not at them
