# Mode 4 · The Liquidity Lord

The Realm runs on its [pools](../physics/liquidity-pools.md) — and the pools pay those who fill them. Mode 4 is capital put to work in the Realm's plumbing itself.

## The Proposition

The Crown built the floors: the Bank's Realm and Eternal bonds are **locked forever** — depth that can never be pulled out from beneath you. The Lab's Sanctioned liquidity stands beside it. **OGs who pool alongside earn the same fee flow on their own positions** — 0.25% on the classic pools, 1% on the V2 Sanctioned set — while their own liquidity remains withdrawable.

Every game's repurchase, every trader's climb, every citizen's entry crosses these pools. Volume in the Realm is fee revenue for its liquidity lords.

## The Lord's Decisions

* **Which pair.** Realm rungs (token-to-token, pure ladder exposure) · Eternal anchors (half outer-world: cbBTC, SOL, USDC) · Sanctioned gates (half SOL, the busiest doors)
* **Which venue.** The same Sanctioned pair exists at 0.25% (V1) and 1% (V2) — fee rate against flow share is its own small game
* **Which weight.** A position is exposure to *both* sides of its pair. Pooling the ladder is a thesis on the ladder; pooling an anchor is a thesis on the border between worlds

## Honest Physics

{% hint style="warning" %}
Liquidity is a position, not a deposit. Pool reserves rebalance against price — what you withdraw will not be the mix you deposited (the outer world calls this impermanent loss). The fee flow is the payment for bearing exactly that. Lords who understand it earn it; tourists subsidize them.
{% endhint %}

## Beyond the Regulated

OGs are also encouraged to found their **own** pools — unique pairs with Realm tokens deepen the Realm and may out-compete the Regulated rates. *If the code allows for it, it is legal in the Realm of OGs.*
