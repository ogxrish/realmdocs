# 🤲 Mode 1 · The Citizen

The simplest mode in the Realm: **hold the floor, and let time work.**

## The Position

Cross the [Citizenship](../society/citizenship.md) floor — 100M $OGC liquid — and stop. No games, no fees, no daily discipline. You are counted, classed 💎, and your [Birth Epoch](../realm/how-to-enter.md) is already aging in your favor.

## Why Passive Is Still a Position

* **Holding aligns you with the Realm.** The value of your bag is tied to the performance of the whole — every miner, reserver, bidder, and builder works the same waters your Coin floats on.
* **The record builds itself.** The [Alchemy Airdrop](../era-2/alchemy-airdrop.md) (UEC 904) judges citizens by gameboard and history. A citizen who merely held — but held early and unbroken — is still a citizen the Realm has known long.
* **The floor is a class with physics.** $OGC's supply still releases from the Reserve every Epoch for years; the commons widen by design. Early floors are cheaper floors.

## The Citizen's Few Decisions

1. **When to enter** — every Epoch of delay is seniority forgone
2. **Whether to climb** — 10× the position is a 🧱 Builder seat; the ladder is open
3. **Whether to play** — any [Mode 3](mode-3-player.md) activity stacks history atop holding; the Realm sees both

{% hint style="info" %}
Citizenship is the mode every other mode contains. Whatever else you become in the Realm, be a citizen first.
{% endhint %}
