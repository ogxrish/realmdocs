# ⚙️ Repurchase Programs

## The Law of Balance

The Repurchase Programs are on-chain programs deployed on Solana mainnet by the Institutions to serve as a balancing mechanism for the flow of [tokens](../physics/tokens.md), which inevitably accelerates over the Realm's lifespan.

The law is one and the same for all three Games: **the fees of play flow back into the token of play.**

## Program Design

Each Repurchase Program is designed to, regularly and automatically:

1. Take possession of fees collected by its Game each Epoch
2. Use those fees to repurchase its Game's token from the open market — routed through Jupiter, taking the most efficient liquidity path
3. Return what it buys to its purpose: **$OGG back into the Mine, $OGC back into the Reserve — and $OGF to the fire**

| Institution | Source | Repurchases | Final Action | Status |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 🛕 OG Mine | Mining fees | $OGG | Replenishes OG Mine | 🟢 **ACTIVE** |
| 🏛️ OG Reserve | Reserve fees | $OGC | Replenishes OG Reserve | 🟠 **UNDER REPAIR** |
| 🎰 OG Lottery | Lottery fees | $OGF | **Burns** the $OGF | ⚪ **COMING** |

**The badges are honest, and they are live.** The Realm audits its own engines: the design above is law, and the status column states plainly which arms are executing onchain today. The Reserve arm is under repair by sovereign order; the Lottery arm is not yet deployed. When an arm fires, its badge turns green — watch the [Onchain Status](../canon/onchain-status.md) page.

## Impact on the Realm

The overall effect of the Repurchase Programs is to assist in supporting a floor value for $OGG, $OGC, and $OGF by continuously repurchasing these tokens from the open market.

Further, this action adds to the daily $OGG Epoch reward emitted by the Mine, and extends the tail-end of Epochs through which the Reserve may continue to emit $OGC.

The Lottery's arm is different in kind: it does **not** extend the Lottery's life. Repurchased $OGF is burned — a pure reduction of excess market supply, and a permanent shrinking of the [Degen class's](../society/classes.md) possible seats.

Since repurchases route through Jupiter, the most efficient liquidity path is always selected — meaning regular repurchases produce a constant rebalancing of the Realm's [pools](../physics/liquidity-pools.md) along the way.
