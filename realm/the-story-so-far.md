# 📜 The Story So Far

## Before the Realm

Before the Realm, there was only the Mad OG — and the raw energy of the real world. He tore a portal into infinite blockspace, and what came through was not merely a game. It was a living cosmos, inscribed forever into the Solana blockchain.

That was the beginning of the **Creation Era** — the first 699 Epochs. It is sealed now. This is what happened inside it.

## Creation — UEC 1

On July 5, 2024, in a single twelve-minute window, the six Realm [tokens](../physics/tokens.md) were struck from `ogrealm.sol` — one $OGR, a thousand $OGM, a million $OGA, a billion $OGG, a trillion $OGC, a quadrillion $OGF. The entire value of the Realm, divided into six forms, in one supply ladder that ascends a thousandfold at every rung.

The day itself went unnamed for seven hundred Epochs. It is named now: **Universal Epoch 1.**

## The Institutions Rise — UEC 2–3

The day after Creation, the Mad OG raised [OG Bank](../power/og-bank.md) and passed it nearly the whole supply of the Realm — the Protector, charged with safeguarding value. The same day, a second institution appeared with no announcement and no explanation: [OG Lab](../power/og-lab.md), the Agency, which took the Realm's alchemy into the shadows.

On the third Epoch the Bank began bonding the tokens to one another — the [Realm Pools](../physics/liquidity-pools/realm-pools.md), locked forever — and in the weeks that followed, to the outer world itself through the [Eternal Pools](../physics/liquidity-pools/eternal-pools.md). The physics of the Realm were set.

## The Gates Open — UEC 26 and 139

At UEC 26, the [OG Mine](../games/og-mine.md) opened — the Giver, emitting 1% of its $OGG every Epoch, forever. The first miners arrived. Some have not missed an Epoch since.

At UEC 139, the [OG Reserve](../games/og-reserve.md) opened — the Allocator, paying a constant river of $OGC to those who lock their hard-won $OGG and play its strange, elegant game: the slot that finishes *second* takes the prize.

A year passed this way. The Discord walls opened to the public. Fees in $OGC arrived. The Knowledge Base and the Realm's first NPC, Jester, came online. Quietly, fortunes were forming.

## The Lottery and the Classes — UEC 500–501

Late in 2025 the Realm's high-stakes Institution was reborn on its own sovereign program: the [OG Lottery](../games/og-lottery.md) — the Gamemaster, testing the primal urges of OGs with accumulating pools of $OGF and the merciless law of the Final Bid.

One Epoch later, the [Classes](../society/classes.md) were announced, and the social order of the Realm became law: OG, Degen, Builder, Capital, Knight, Noble — and above them, gates not yet released. Players were no longer merely holders. They had rank.

In the months that followed, the first 💎 commoners climbed, the first 🛡️ Knights assembled their bonds and took seats at the court, and the great bags of the Realm — some public, some shadowed — took the shapes they hold today.

## The Clock Revealed — UEC 700

On June 4, 2026, the Mad OG revealed what had been running silently since the first block: the **Universal Epoch Clock**, the Realm's single sovereign calendar. Every emission, every lock, every bid had always happened inside a numbered Epoch — now the numbers were spoken.

With the same words, the Creation Era was sealed, and the next era began.

> *Epoch 700 begins the next.*

Era II has not been named. It holds, among other things, the [Alchemy Airdrop](../era-2/alchemy-airdrop.md) — UEC 904, December 25, 2026 — when the Realm will judge its own gameboard and history, and reward those it has known best and longest.
