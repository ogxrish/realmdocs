# 🕋 OG Lab

## Created by Mad OG

[Mad OG](mad-og.md) created OG Lab, a mysterious institution for which very little is known. It appeared on UEC Epoch 2 — the same day as the Bank — with no announcement and no explanation. It is the **Agency** pillar of the Crown: where the Bank holds, the Lab acts.

Though veiled in mystery and obscurity, it is not wise to question Mad OG's intentions when erecting the Institutions of the Realm.

## Initial Funding

To establish OG Lab, Mad OG securely transferred a classified amount of SOL for initial funding, while [OG Bank](og-bank.md) entrusted it with the great share of [$OGA](../physics/tokens/oga.md) — **80% of the supply in total** (70% at the Lab's founding, a further 10% in May 2026). The dark science of the Realm has been in its keeping ever since.

## What the Chain Has Seen

The Lab does not explain itself. But the chain records what it does:

* **It built the [Sanctioned Pools](../physics/liquidity-pools/sanctioned-pools.md)** — unannounced — giving every Realm token a direct path to native SOL liquidity, in V1 and again in V2
* **It buys.** In December 2024 the Lab market-purchased 5% of the entire $OGG supply in the open — and it has accumulated since ([proof](../realm/timeline.md))
* **It seeded the entire [OG Airdrop](../era-2/alchemy-airdrop.md)** — the whole of the Airdrop's endowment (500,000 $OGA, **50% of the supply**) was supplied from the Lab's own vaults, in preparation for the Alchemy Airdrop at UEC 904

## The 30% Floor

The arithmetic closes cleanly: the Lab was endowed **80%** of $OGA, supplied **50%** to the Airdrop, and **retains 30%** — the Realm's understood floor of institutional control ([The Crown's Weight](../society/classes.md)). The Bank's 10% top-up, two days after the Airdrop seeding, is what restored the Lab to exactly that floor. The Agency gives the supply to the people, yet keeps the weight that secures its domain.

## Source of Income

OG Lab earns fees on the unlocked liquidity it provides in the Sanctioned Pools. Unlike the Bank's eternal bonds, the Lab's liquidity is active — placed, adjusted, and removable at its discretion. Support for the ecosystem, on the Agency's terms.
