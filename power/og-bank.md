# 🏦 OG Bank

## Established by Mad OG

Upon creating the 6-token structure for the Realm, [Mad OG](mad-og.md) established the OG Bank to protect the value of the Realm. It was the first Institution — raised on UEC Epoch 2, the day after Creation — and it is the **Permanence** pillar of the Crown.

## Initial Funding

The total supply of all 6 [tokens](../physics/tokens.md) was securely transferred to OG Bank, with the exception of [$OGR](../physics/tokens/ogr.md), of which Mad OG retained 90% — safeguarding nearly all of the power of the Realm. Every transfer is [on the chain](../realm/timeline.md).

## The Great Endowments

The Bank did not scatter the supply. It held each token's endowment in trust, and released it only when its purpose came of age:

What the Bank retains, it retains deliberately: **70% of all $OGM, 10% of $OGG, and 10% of $OGF** rest in its vaults to this day — the strategic reserves of the Realm.

What the Bank does not retain or endow, it bonds into the Realm's [pools](../physics/liquidity-pools.md) (detailed there). **The $OGA endowment is the one to follow closely:** the Bank passed **80% of $OGA to the [Lab](og-lab.md)** in two stages — 70% at the Lab's founding, and a further 10% in May 2026, two days after the Lab seeded the [Alchemy Airdrop](../era-2/alchemy-airdrop.md). That top-up was deliberate. The Lab had just sent 50% of the supply to the Airdrop wallet; the Bank's 10% restored it to a **30% holding — the Realm's understood floor of institutional control** (see [The Crown's Weight](../society/classes.md)). The remaining 20% of $OGA sits bonded in the Realm pools — and the whole supply closes: 50% to the people, 30% to the Lab, 20% locked in the pools.

## Realm Liquidity

Ordained as the Protector of the Realm's value, it became the Bank's duty to oversee the critical frameworks of the Realm's token liquidity.

**Token bonding:** the Bank formed the first and most significant physical bonds of the Realm — the [Realm Pools](../physics/liquidity-pools/realm-pools.md) (10% of each supply, bonded pair to pair) and the [Eternal Pools](../physics/liquidity-pools/eternal-pools.md) (bonds to cbBTC, SOL, and USDC) — and **burned the LP tokens, locking the principal forever.**

## Source of Income

The Bank's locked principal can never be removed — **but its fee income is not locked.** Fees earned by the Realm and Eternal Pools accrue to OG Bank and are claimable at any time. To this day, the Bank has never claimed them. They are its primary source of income, and the Realm expects the Bank to draw upon them in some way, at some time of its choosing.

Since the token balances of these pools represent the core flow of value across the Realm and are so large relative to the overall supply, volume in the Realm translates directly to fee revenue for OG Bank. OGs who pool alongside the Bank earn that same fee flow on their own positions — atop a foundation that can never be pulled out from under them.

## Creation of OG Reserve

OG Bank created [OG Reserve](../games/og-reserve.md) to allow for the emergence of $OGC, the common value of the Realm. Although not a source of income for the Bank, the Reserve allows the value of $OGG to flow organically into $OGC — benefiting the expansion of the Realm's holder base and the distribution of its value.
