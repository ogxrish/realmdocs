# Knowledge Base

## Information Database

The Knowledge Base is the central intelligence hub of the Realm — a storage facility for the growing complex of information generated by the Realm's activities, accessible to humans and non-humans alike.

**This book is its foundation.** Every page of the docs flows automatically into the Knowledge Base when it changes; the canon you are reading and the canon the Realm's machines consult are one and the same.

## Contents

1. **The Docs** — this book ([docs.ogrealm.com](https://docs.ogrealm.com)), verified and stamped page by page
2. **The machine layer** — the [Verified Registry](verified-registry.md), [Onchain Status](onchain-status.md), and the canon JSON behind the [Agent's Gate](agents-gate.md)

Over time, the contents of the Knowledge Base will grow with the Realm.

## NPCs (Non-Player Characters)

NPCs are characters controlled not by an OG, but by the Realm's own intelligence — essential to the story, helpful to players, and woven into the game world.

### 🃏 Jester

Jester is the Realm's first NPC. He has access to the entire Knowledge Base and is accessible in [Discord](https://discord.gg/ogrealm).

> Jester is your typical, lovable town idiot.
>
> He hangs out in the Arena and loves to interact with anyone who chooses to engage.
>
> He's generally pretty jolly, mostly helpful, and loves to share and impress.
>
> But make no mistake, Jester isn't actually an idiot.
>
> He's been around the Realm for a very long time.
>
> And he can enlighten you on the ins and outs — if you know what to ask.

Jester runs v0.3, with live price awareness and the full canon at his fingertips. His abilities improve with subsequent versions — and because the docs feed him automatically, **he is never older than the last stamped page.**
