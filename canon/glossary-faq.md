# Glossary & FAQ

## FAQ

**How do I start?** [How to Enter](../realm/how-to-enter.md) — wallet, first $OGC, citizenship at 100M, Discord. Thirty minutes, start to finish.

**Do I need to play to benefit?** No — [Mode 1](../paths/mode-1-citizen.md) is holding alone. But the Realm judges history, and players write more of it.

**What happens if I don't claim rewards?** After 10 Epochs: Mine and Reserve rewards return to their Institutions; Lottery winnings **burn**. Claim.

**Is the liquidity really locked?** The Bank's principal in Realm and Eternal Pools is burned-locked forever — [proof on the Timeline](../realm/timeline.md). Its fee income is claimable; the Lab's Sanctioned liquidity is removable. Each pool page says which.

**Why doesn't my staked Gold count for Class?** The [Liquid Law](../society/classes.md): commitment and standing are a visible trade. The Reserve pays you $OGC for exactly what the Class ladder stops seeing.

**How are fees calculated?** [One law](../physics/difficulty.md), all games: the fee rises with each player in the period — early is cheap, crowds pay. The exact formula lives on the Difficulty page.

**Who is the Mad OG?** [The Creator](../power/mad-og.md). He speaks only behind the Realm's walls — and on the chain, where everything he built is signed.

**Is this financial advice?** The Realm gives laws, proofs, and history — never advice. *No one is required to enter the Realm of OGs. Anyone who wishes to test their fate, certainly can.*
